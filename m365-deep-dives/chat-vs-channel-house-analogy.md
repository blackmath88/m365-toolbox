# Chat vs. channel: the house analogy

> Status: teaching idea from Miro, not a sourced finding. It complements `report.md`, where the chat/channel comparison is verified against Microsoft Learn (see section 1.2 there).

## The idea

| Teams element | Analogy | What it means for learners |
|---|---|---|
| Team | The house | The people who belong together |
| Channel | A room in the house, with a purpose | Everyone who belongs can walk in, see what happened, and pick up where others left off |
| Posts and replies | Conversation in that room | Visible to the whole team and tied to one topic |
| Files | Shared objects in the room | Everyone works on the same thing, not on copies carried around |
| Chat | A conversation outside the house | Asking a neighbour how they are doing, or going out to eat with friends: informal, a few people, not tied to the work in the rooms |

## Rule of thumb for the session

"If someone who joins later would need to know it, say it in the room (channel). If it is just between us right now, go outside (chat)."

## Why it supports the mental-model shift

- It replaces "the file moves between people" with "people gather around the same object in the same room".
- It explains why new team members can read the history: they move into the house and the rooms already have a record.
- It gives a simple answer to "where do I write this?"

## Caveats to check before using it

- The analogy must not imply that chat is unimportant. Chat is the right place for quick, private or temporary coordination.
- It must not imply that channels are always better. Too many rooms make a house hard to navigate.
- Test it with a few colleagues first. It may land differently in German, so draft a German version for the Basel audience.
- Confirm the facts behind it against current Microsoft documentation (visibility, threading, history for new members), as `report.md` section 1.2 notes.

## Possible use in the 45-minute session

Use it in the "Map" block (about minutes 7-14): draw the house on one slide, then reuse the same picture in the scenario demo.
